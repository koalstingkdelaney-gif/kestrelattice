Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 5, 2026

ghostcorpnet wants this site to be usable by everyone. We are partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 AA and are working toward full conformance.

What we ship

  • Skip link. A "Skip to content" link on the main pages lets keyboard users jump straight past navigation.
  • Visible focus rings. Links, buttons, and summaries show a high-contrast 3px outline on keyboard focus (:focus-visible), including product cards via :focus-within.
  • Reduced-motion support. prefers-reduced-motion is honored: animations, smooth scrolling, and transitions are disabled or simplified when you request it.
  • 44px tap targets. Buttons, nav links, and footer links meet the 44px minimum touch-target size.
  • Alt text on images. Product imagery and social preview images carry descriptive alt text.
  • Keyboard-navigable FAQ. FAQ sections use native <details> elements — operable by keyboard with no script required.
  • Readable defaults. Dark high-contrast palette, generous line-height, and visible focus everywhere you can interact.

Known gaps

Some pages are still being audited. If you run into anything we missed — unlabeled controls, keyboard traps, color-contrast problems — we'd genuinely like to know.

Feedback

Email corpghost420@gmail.com with "Accessibility feedback" in the subject line. We read every report and prioritize fixes.
