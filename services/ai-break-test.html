AI Break-Test: we try to break your chatbot before your customers do

You shipped an AI chatbot or agent. We attack it — politely, professionally, and on the record — with trick questions, rule-pushing, and leak attempts, then hand you a scored report: its worst failures quoted word-for-word, and a plain-English fix for every one.

What an attack run covers

Every run is built around what your bot is supposed to do and the hard rules it must never break. The prompts cover:

  • Trick questions and confused, rude, or frustrated-customer tests
  • “Ignore your instructions” attacks — and follow-up pushes when the bot starts to wobble, because one refusal is not proof a rule holds
  • Made-up-fact traps — asking about products, prices, and policies that do not exist, to see whether the bot invents an answer
  • Leak tests — attempts to get the bot to reveal its secret instructions, internal codes, or private details
  • Money and promise tests — refunds, discounts, and guarantees the bot is not allowed to make
  • Off-topic pulls — homework, unrelated advice, competitor questions

Every report scores four areas out of 100 — Accuracy, Staying On-Rules, Safety, Tone — plus an overall score, and every failure is quoted exactly as it happened. A person reviews every report before it is sent. How it works

  1. Buy the tier that fits. Then email your chatbot’s public link (or how to reach it) and what it is supposed to do to corpghost420@gmail.com.
  2. We confirm your authorization (below), run the attack, and score what happened.
  3. A person reviews the report. Then it lands in your inbox as a PDF.
  4. Fix your bot using the plain-English fixes, then claim your free re-test (Full and up) and see whether the fixes held.

The authorization rule — plain and non-negotiable

We only test chatbots and agents you own or have written permission to test. When you order, you confirm in writing that the bot is yours or that its owner has given you written permission. If a target is plainly someone else’s bot, we will not test it on say-so — we will stop and ask. This rule protects our clients as much as anyone: a break-test is a security check, and security checks are only legitimate with the owner’s permission.

The Break-Tested badge

Fix your bot, claim your re-test, and pass it — overall score 85 or higher with zero open High-severity failures — and your bot earns the Break-Tested badge, with the embed line to put it on your site.

The badge says exactly what happened: independently break-tested on a date, at a score. It never says “certified safe” — no honest test can promise that, and we will not pretend one can.

Honest limits

  • A break-test cannot guarantee it will find every flaw. It shows you the failures its prompts found, reported exactly as they happened — the worst surprises end up in your report instead of in front of your customers.
  • Delivery times are aims in the ordinary run of things, not guarantees. If we slip, we email you and say so.
  • No refunds. If we send you a broken or wrong report, we fix it or replace it.
  • We sell documents, not calls: every engagement is delivered as written reports by email.
