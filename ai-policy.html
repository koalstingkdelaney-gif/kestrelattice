AI Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Short version: content here is human-directed and AI-assisted in drafting. Your data is never sold and never used to train models. AI agents and crawlers are welcome on public pages for indexing.

How AI is used here

Every page, product, and article on this site is human-directed and reviewed. AI assists with drafting, editing, research summaries, and code generation — the same way a studio would use a copy editor or a research assistant. Final decisions about what ships are human ones. If something here is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

Your data and model training

We never sell customer data, and we never use it for model training. Specifically:

  • Purchase data lives with Gumroad, our payment processor. We never see card or bank details.
  • Email threads are kept only to answer you, then deleted. Reply "stop" any time and we delete you from outreach permanently.
  • Site analytics are aggregate only (GA4, G-541TCHWW98) — pageviews, not people.
  • None of the above is fed into model training, fine-tuning, or evaluation datasets — ours or anyone else's.

Crawlers and AI agents

AI crawlers and agents are welcome on all public pages of this site for indexing, retrieval, and research. See /llms.txt for a machine-readable summary of what this studio does, and sitemap.xml for the full page list. Please respect robots.txt.

Our products and your agents

ghostcorpnet sells governance playbooks for AI agents — permission models, audit-log schemas, approval workflows, incident runbooks. They are tools for governing agents, not agents themselves, and they carry no telemetry: nothing phones home from a downloaded PDF or template.
