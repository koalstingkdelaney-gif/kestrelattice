About ghostcorpnet Studio

ghostcorpnet is an independent studio building self-serve governance playbooks for teams shipping AI agents to production — identity, spend limits, approval gates, and audit trails, packaged as files you can use today.

What we publish

Every ghostcorpnet product is a self-serve digital file — policy templates, runbooks, checklists, and worksheets you download and apply yourself. No calls, no onboarding, no consulting required. Flagship products include The Playbook — Studio Edition ($29) and the free MIT-licensed Governed Agent Mesh Playbook.

Eleven specialist stores cover the niches where agent governance matters most — incident response, customer support, education, finance, healthcare, hiring, law, sourcing, civic tech, and frontier research.

How we work

  • Governance-first: every product enforces the mesh discipline — what an agent did, spent, and was allowed to do must always be answerable.
  • Self-serve by design: buy, download, apply. Everything is documented and template-driven.
  • 30-day money-back guarantee on paid products via Gumroad — no questions asked.
