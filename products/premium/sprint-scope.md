# Guided Implementation Sprint — Scope of Work

**Price: $6,000 one-time · Timeline: 4 weeks · Delivered by Kestrelattice (GhostCorp)**

We install the governed agent mesh in your stack with you: the 14-day rollout from the Studio Edition, executed against your real agents, with weekly working sessions and async support. You end with governance that's live, verified, and signed off — not a plan in a doc.

## What's included

1. **Week 0 — Inventory & plan** — full actor inventory, identity registry stood up, rollout scheduled against your stack.
2. **Weeks 1–2 — Foundation** — grant store, least-privilege pass, hash-linked audit log, cost ceilings + metering on every plan. Built in your repo, in your tooling.
3. **Weeks 3–4 — Control** — approval gates live, draft-only paths separated, warning-to-block sweep, red-team exercise against the finished stack.
4. **Weekly working sessions (4 × 90 min)** — we build with your engineers, not at them. Sessions are recorded for your team.
5. **Async support** — questions answered within 1 business day for the sprint duration.
6. **Acceptance sign-off** — the Studio Edition acceptance checklist completed, signed, and archived with your audit log.
7. **Handover doc** — runbooks for the ongoing weekly rhythm: chain verification, quarterly policy review, re-tiering triggers.

## What we need from you

- 1–2 engineers who can give ~5 hrs/week during the sprint (pairing, access, decisions).
- Ability to grant the access your own engineers already have (we work inside your permissions, never around them).
- A named owner who can sign off on tier decisions and the final acceptance checklist.

## Terms

- 50% ($3,000) to book, 50% at the week-2 midpoint.
- Fixed 4-week scope. If your team stalls (no access, no pairing time), the clock pauses — we don't bill for your delay, and we don't deliver without your engineers in the room.
- We never ask for production credentials or secrets; we work with your engineers on screenshare where needed.
- Everything we build lives in your repo under your ownership. No lock-in, no license fees.

## What this is not

- Not a substitute for your engineers — your team must show up; we guide and build alongside.
- Not legal/compliance certification. It's production-grade governance practice, verified by red-team.
- We don't sell tooling. If the vendor matrix says buy a meter, you buy it from the vendor.
