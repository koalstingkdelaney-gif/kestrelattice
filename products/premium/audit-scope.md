# Agent Governance Audit — Scope of Work

**Price: $1,500 one-time · Timeline: 2 weeks · Delivered by Kestrelattice (GhostCorp)**

A professional audit of your AI agent stack against the governed-agent-mesh framework: eight layers, three risk tiers, fail-closed controls. You get a written findings report and a prioritized remediation roadmap — not a slide deck of generic advice.

## What's included

1. **Stack inventory & identity review** — every agent, script, cron job, and scheduled workflow that can take action in your stack, checked for owner-scoped identity and registry coverage.
2. **Permission & grant audit** — every agent permission evaluated against least-privilege; permanent or over-broad grants flagged with the exact grant to shrink.
3. **Risk-tier classification** — every capability tiered Hard-gated / Bounded-auto / Draft-only, with mis-tiered actions called out (especially "read-only" actions with write-grade consequences).
4. **Spend-control review** — per-plan cost ceilings, expiry rules, metering coverage, and fail-closed behavior checked; runaway-loop scenarios identified.
5. **Audit-trail assessment** — whether every request, approval, block, and outcome writes a tamper-evident row, and whether anyone could verify it.
6. **Findings report + remediation roadmap** — every finding scored by severity, sequenced into a 30/60/90-day fix order with the exact evidence each fix requires.
7. **Readout call (60 min)** — walkthrough of findings with your team, Q&A included.

## What we need from you

- Read access to agent configurations, permission/grant records, and (where they exist) logs — via screenshare or read-only access, your choice.
- One 60-minute kickoff call and one 60-minute readout call.
- A named technical contact who can answer stack questions within 2 business days during the audit.

## What this is not

- Not implementation — fixes are your team's (or book the Implementation Sprint).
