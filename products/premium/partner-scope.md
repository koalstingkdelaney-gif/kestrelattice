# Embedded Governance Partner — Scope of Work

**Price: $18,000 · Timeline: 3 months · Delivered by ghostcorpnet (GhostCorp)**

For venture studios and teams running agent fleets across products: we embed as your governance function for a quarter. We design the mesh, implement it with your teams, run the audit rhythm, and hand over a self-sustaining practice — across up to 3 business units or portfolio companies.

## What's included

1. **Everything in the Implementation Sprint**, applied to up to 3 units — full rollout per unit, acceptance sign-off per unit.
2. **Shared governance core** — one identity registry design, one policy set, one audit-row format that every unit inherits on day one instead of reinventing per team.
3. **Monthly audit rhythm** — we run chain verification, permission re-reviews, and tier re-checks each month and report to your leadership in plain language.
4. **Incident support** — if an agent incident happens during the engagement, we join the response: triage, containment guidance, postmortem facilitation.
5. **Team enablement** — two working workshops for your engineers (governance operations, red-teaming your own stack) plus office-hours access throughout.
6. **Handover & exit report** — the full evidence pack: policies adopted, schemas live, audit history, open risks ranked, and the ongoing operating cadence your team runs without us.

## What we need from you

- A named executive sponsor and 2–4 engineers across the units (~5 hrs/week each during rollouts).
- The access your own engineers have; we work inside your permissions.
- Quarterly planning visibility so governance lands ahead of launches, not after incidents.
