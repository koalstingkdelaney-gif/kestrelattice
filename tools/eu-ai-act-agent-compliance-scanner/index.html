Introduction

AI agents are not ordinary software features. An agent that reads data, calls tools, and acts on its own sits squarely inside the EU AI Act's risk-based duties — risk management, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping, transparency, human oversight, and post-market monitoring. This scanner turns those duties into a concrete checklist for one deployment: describe the agent, tick the controls you actually have, and get a risk score plus the exact gaps to close, in priority order.

Results and Recommendations

Your score weights each missing requirement by how much it matters for your risk profile: a missing audit trail or missing human oversight on a high-risk, autonomous agent scores worse than the same gap on a minimal-risk drafting tool. Work the checklist top-down — critical gaps first — and re-run the scan whenever the agent, its autonomy, its data, or its use case changes. Compliance decays silently as agents gain new tools and new data access.

  • Risk management: a documented process for identifying and mitigating the agent's foreseeable risks, kept current across its lifecycle.
  • Data governance: documented sources, rights, lineage, and retention for the data the agent reads and learns from.
  • Technical documentation: architecture, intended use, known limits, and evaluation results, ready for a regulator or enterprise buyer.
  • Record-keeping: timestamped logs of agent actions, approvals, overrides, and stops, under a retention policy.
  • Transparency: people interacting with the agent — or affected by its output — know it, and know when a human can step in.
  • Human oversight: named overseers who understand the system, can interpret its output, and can override or stop it.

FAQ

Is this legal advice?

No. This tool is an informational self-assessment for operational readiness — it is not legal advice and does not determine your legal classification under the EU AI Act.

Does anything I enter leave my device?

No. The scan runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing you enter is sent to a server.

What is the buyer keyword this tool answers?

EU AI Act compliance checklist — the scan generates exactly that: a prioritised compliance checklist with risk scoring for an AI agent deployment.

How is risk scoring calculated?

Each unticked requirement counts as a gap, weighted by requirement importance and by your use-case, autonomy, and data selections. The score is the weighted share of requirements in place, out of 100.
