Next Steps

Fix red cells first: they combine a legal duty (Article 10 or 14) with the widest exposure from your risk tier, scale, and residency. Then work the amber cells, document what you fixed, and re-run this calculator whenever the system, its scale, or its data flows change — compliance gaps reopen silently as deployments grow.

  • Article 10: training, validation, and test data must be relevant, representative, and examined for bias, with data-governance practices documented.
  • Article 14: high-risk systems must be effectively overseen by named humans who can monitor, interpret, override, and stop them.

FAQ

Is this legal advice?

No. This tool is an informational self-assessment for operational readiness — it is not legal advice.

Does my input leave my device?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. The share link encodes only the selections you made here.

What is a heatmap cell?

Each cell is one compliance area. Its colour combines whether the underlying control is present with your risk category, deployment scale, and data residency — missing controls hurt more at higher risk and scale.
