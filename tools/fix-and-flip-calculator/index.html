Common questions

What is the 70% rule?

A conservative guideline: max allowable offer = 70% of ARV minus repair costs. The 30% margin covers profit, holding costs, selling costs, and the surprises every renovation hides.

What ROI should I target?

Many experienced flippers want at least a 15–20% profit margin on ARV, or a minimum dollar profit (often $20,000+) that justifies the risk and the months of work.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's arithmetic on the numbers you type. Verify ARV with real comparable sales, get contractor bids for repairs, and do your own due diligence before spending money.

Does my data leave my device?

No. Every calculation happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
