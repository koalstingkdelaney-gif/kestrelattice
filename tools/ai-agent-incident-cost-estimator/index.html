How the math works

Incidents per month = agents × incidents per agent per month. Monthly handling hours = incidents per month × hours per incident. Monthly cost = handling hours × your hourly loaded cost, and the annual figure is that × 12. With the no-audit-trail toggle on, hours per incident are multiplied by (1 + your extra-time slider) — the slider is your assumption about how much longer reconstruction makes an investigation, and it is labeled that way because it is one. No averages from anyone else's fleet are baked in anywhere.

The two levers that lower this number

  • Fewer incidents: bounds and approval gates stop agents from taking the actions that become incidents in the first place — a bounded agent can't wander into the expensive failure modes. Try lowering the incidents field above to what a bounded setup would look like for your team and compare.
  • Faster investigations: an audit trail — a durable log of what each agent did, when, and under whose approval — turns reconstruction into reading. Flip the toggle above off and the difference is what your own assumption says the trail is worth.
  • Cheaper hours: runbooks and a clear incident record mean the first responder doesn't have to be the most senior person on the team, which lowers the hourly rate in the last field honestly — not by paying people less, but by needing less of their time per incident.
