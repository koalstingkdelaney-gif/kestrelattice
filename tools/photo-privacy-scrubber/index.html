Why this matters

Every photo your phone takes is stamped with EXIF metadata. Post an original photo online or send it to someone, and you're handing over the GPS coordinates of your home, your kid's school, or wherever you were standing — plus your exact phone model and the timestamp. Scrubbing takes ten seconds and removes all of it.

FAQ

What hidden data do photos contain?

Most phone photos carry EXIF metadata: GPS coordinates of where the shot was taken, the phone make and model, and the exact date and time. Anyone you send the original file to can read it.

Do my photos leave my device when I use this tool?

No. The scrubber runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Does scrubbing reduce photo quality?

Slightly. Scrubbed photos are re-encoded at high quality (92%), which is visually identical for sharing and posting. The original file on your device is never modified.

Which formats are supported?

JPEG and PNG photos, including HEIC-converted files saved as JPEG/PNG. iPhone users should export as JPEG first for full metadata inspection.
