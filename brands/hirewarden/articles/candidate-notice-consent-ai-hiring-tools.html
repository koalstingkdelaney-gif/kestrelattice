Candidate notice and consent for AI hiring tools: what to tell applicants

Published 2026-10-01 ·

As the hiring process transitions towards more efficient and effective AI tools, it is essential for HR professionals and recruiters to understand the candidate notice and consent requirements. In this guide, we will discuss the essential elements of a candidate notice and consent for AI hiring tools, focusing on best practices for fair hiring.

Understanding Candidate Notice and Consent

Candidate notice refers to the information provided to applicants about the use of AI in the hiring process. Consent, on the other hand, is the applicant's agreement to the use of their data for AI hiring tools.

Provide Clear Information About AI in Hiring

To ensure transparency, clearly communicate to candidates about the use of AI in the hiring process. Here are some tips:

  • Include a brief explanation of AI and how it will be used for hiring in your company.
  • Mention any limitations or potential biases associated with AI tools.
  • Provide information on how candidate data will be collected, processed, and used.

Ensure Candidate Consent

To get candidate consent, follow these guidelines:

  • Provide clear and concise language for consent forms or notices.
  • Ensure that candidates have the option to choose whether they want to participate in AI-assisted hiring.
  • Mention any potential consequences of declining consent, such as limited access to features or services.

Address Privacy and Data Protection

When using AI in hiring, addressing privacy and data protection is crucial:

  • Ensure candidate data is collected, processed, and stored securely.
  • Explain how candidate data will be used and what happens to the data after the hiring process.

Ensure Fairness and Equality

To ensure fairness and equality in hiring processes:

  • Explain the purpose of AI-assisted hiring, emphasizing its goal to improve hiring decisions.
  • Highlight the measures taken to prevent AI-related biases, such as training AI systems on diverse datasets and monitoring AI performance.

Provide Candidate Choice

To ensure candidates have choices regarding AI-assisted hiring:

  • Give candidates the opportunity to opt-in or opt-out of AI-assisted hiring.
  • Provide information about the types of data AI will utilize and its purpose.

Communicate About AI Assisted Hiring

Communicate clearly about AI-assisted hiring:

  • Candidates should be informed about AI involvement in the hiring process.
  • Make sure to explain how AI is used for tasks such as screening resumes, interview scheduling, and candidate profiling.

Follow GDPR Guidelines

When using AI, it is essential to adhere to GDPR guidelines:

  • Explain how candidate data is collected, processed, and utilized.
  • Provide transparent information about AI systems, including how they learn and improve over time.

Consider Candidate Preferences

To ensure a positive candidate experience, consider their preferences:

  • Give candidates the option to decline AI-assisted recruitment methods.
  • Inform candidates about the availability of human review if they prefer human interaction.

Address Candidate Data Privacy

Data privacy is a top priority:

  • Communicate openly about how candidate data will be processed and protected.
  • Ensure that AI systems are regularly audited to avoid bias and discrimination.

Inform Candidates About AI-Assisted Interviews

Be transparent about AI-assisted interviews:

  • Inform candidates about AI-assisted interviews, including details such as how AI will be used during interviews and its potential impact on the interview process.
  • Ensure candidates are aware of the rights they have, including the right to opt-out of AI-assisted interviews.

Ensure Transparency in AI-Assisted Screening

Maintain transparency throughout the hiring process:

  • Communicate how AI technologies are used for screening and how it helps to improve efficiency and reduce bias.
  • Be transparent about the data used to train AI systems and the steps taken to avoid biases.
