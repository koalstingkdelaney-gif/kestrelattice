Reconciling agent-made payments: a daily ledger review routine

Published 2026-10-01 ·

As a financial services firm, maintaining accurate records and ensuring the smooth flow of transactions is crucial. One crucial aspect of this involves reconciling agent-made payments, which ensures that all transactions are properly recorded and accounted for. In this guide, we will discuss the daily ledger review routine that practitioners can adopt to streamline the payment reconciliation process.

Step 1: Define Roles and Responsibilities

The first step towards reconciling agent-made payments is defining the roles and responsibilities of the involved parties. This includes:

  • Understanding the responsibilities of payment agents and approvers.
  • Defining the process for reviewing and approving payments.
  • Establishing clear communication channels and escalation procedures.

Step 2: Establish Payment Review Protocols

Once roles and responsibilities are defined, it is essential to establish payment review protocols. These protocols will guide the daily ledger review process and ensure consistency and accuracy:

  • Creating a daily payment run: Prepare a list of all payments to be processed each day.
  • Reviewing payments against payment criteria: Ensure that all payments meet the required criteria, such as amount, recipient, and purpose.
  • Verifying payment details: Check the payment details against the original source document, such as customer orders, purchase orders, or supplier invoices.
  • Approving or rejecting payments: Approve payments that meet the criteria and verify details, while rejecting payments that do not.
  • Escalating issues: Identify any issues or discrepancies and escalate them to the appropriate person for resolution.
  • Follow up on outstanding payments: Determine the status of outstanding payments and take appropriate action.

Step 3: Conduct Daily Ledger Review

Conducting a daily ledger review is crucial for identifying and resolving discrepancies promptly. The following steps outline the process:

  • Retrieve payment data: Gather the payment information for review, including payment details, approvals, and payment status.
  • Check payment details against source documents: Compare the payment details with the original source documents, such as purchase orders, invoices, or customer orders.
  • Verify payment approvals: Ensure that all payments have been properly approved, following the defined payment review protocol.
  • Review payment status: Assess the status of payments, such as whether they are pending, approved, or rejected.
  • Identify discrepancies: Identify any discrepancies or issues that require further investigation.

Step 4: Conduct Regular Audits

Regular audits play a vital role in ensuring the accuracy and integrity of financial records. Conducting audits helps identify potential risks and enhances the overall financial management process. The following steps outline the audit process:

  • Choose a sampling method: Select a sampling method to ensure a representative sample of transactions.
  • Check for accuracy and completeness: Review the selected transactions to ensure that they are accurate and complete.
  • Evaluate internal controls: Evaluate the effectiveness of internal controls to prevent errors and fraud.
  • Identify potential risks: Look for potential risks or irregularities, such as duplicate payments or missing payments.

Step 5: Establish a Monitoring and Reporting Framework

An effective monitoring and reporting framework helps ensure that financial processes are in line with internal controls and regulatory requirements. The following steps outline the framework:

  • Define monitoring criteria: Define the criteria that will be used to monitor the financial processes.
  • Reporting structure: Establish a reporting structure for monitoring and reporting purposes.
  • Ensure compliance with regulations: Ensure that the monitoring and reporting framework complies with relevant regulations and industry standards.
  • Develop a reporting schedule: Establish a regular reporting schedule to monitor and report on financial processes.
  • Create action plans: Develop action plans to address any identified issues or non-compliance.
  • Implement monitoring and reporting processes: Implement monitoring and reporting processes to ensure that financial processes meet internal controls and regulatory requirements.

Step 6: Develop a Control Environment

Establishing a strong control environment is essential for ensuring the integrity of financial processes. The following steps outline the key elements of a sound control environment:

  • Identify control objectives: Define the objectives of the control environment to align with business goals and objectives.
  • Establish controls: Establish controls to meet the identified control objectives.
  • Monitor and measure control effectiveness: Monitor and measure the effectiveness of the implemented controls regularly.
  • Address control weaknesses: Identify and address any identified control weaknesses promptly.
  • Continuously improve controls: Continuously review and refine the control environment based on lessons learned and feedback from employees, customers, and other stakeholders.
