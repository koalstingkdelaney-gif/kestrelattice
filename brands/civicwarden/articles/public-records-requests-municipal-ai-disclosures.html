Public records requests for AI systems: preparing municipal AI disclosures

Published 2026-10-01 ·

In today's era of technological advancements, artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly being adopted by public sector organizations to improve efficiency, decision-making, and citizen services. However, ensuring transparency and accountability in the deployment of AI systems is crucial to maintain public trust. One aspect of public-sector transparency is addressing public records requests, particularly when it comes to AI systems in the municipal context. With the growing demand for information about AI systems by citizens, municipalities need to prepare for these requests and ensure effective disclosure.

Understanding Public Records Requests

Public records requests are requests made by citizens to access information held by public authorities. These requests are typically made to ensure transparency and accountability, and to help citizens understand how their local government operates. In the context of AI systems, public records requests can pertain to information about the development, deployment, and evaluation of AI applications.

Preparing for Public Records Requests on Municipal AI Systems

As municipalities adopt AI systems, they should anticipate public records requests concerning these technologies. To effectively prepare for such requests, municipalities should:

  • Establish a clear policy on access to AI-related records: Develop a standardized policy outlining the information that can be disclosed, how requests will be processed, and any limitations or exemptions. This policy should be easily accessible and communicated to relevant stakeholders.
  • Educate personnel responsible for handling requests: Provide training to ensure that those handling records requests understand the policy and can effectively process requests.
  • Establish a dedicated records request process: Create a structured process for handling records requests, including designated personnel, timelines for response, and procedures for handling exempt information.
  • Consider involving legal counsel: Engage legal counsel to advise on compliance with laws and regulations related to public records requests.
  • Establish procedures for evaluating AI-related requests: Determine which AI-related records fall within the scope of public records requests and establish guidelines for evaluating these requests.

Managing Public Records Requests on Municipal AI Systems

Once you have prepared for public records requests, the next step is to manage these requests effectively:

  • Educate citizens about AI systems: Provide transparent information about your municipal AI systems, including their objectives, scope, and potential impacts on citizens. This proactive communication will help citizens better understand and navigate the process of public records requests.
  • Create a comprehensive records request form: Develop a user-friendly form to streamline the process for both citizens and municipal staff. Include sections for requestor information, purpose of the request, and specific information being sought.
  • Develop a standard response template: Prepare a standard response template addressing common questions and concerns about municipal AI systems. This template can be used to respond to similar requests, ensuring consistency and clarity.
  • Establish guidelines for handling exempt information: Create guidelines for handling exempt information, such as trade secrets or personally identifiable information, to protect both citizens and the municipality while fulfilling requests.
  • Define timelines and processes: Define clear timelines for processing requests and establish procedures for prioritizing and fulfilling requests promptly while ensuring accuracy and completeness.

Meeting Citizen Expectations: Best Practices for Municipal AI Disclosures

Public trust is crucial for the successful implementation and adoption of municipal AI systems. To meet citizens' expectations and ensure transparency, municipalities should:

  • Develop a comprehensive disclosure policy: Craft a policy that outlines the types of records that are subject to disclosure, as well as exemptions and limitations.
  • Engage with citizens: Establish channels for citizen feedback and engagement, such as town hall meetings or a dedicated AI disclosure portal.
  • Include AI-related information in municipal disclosure initiatives: Integrate AI-related information into existing transparency initiatives, such as annual reports or citizen engagement platforms.
  • Educate AI system developers and operators: Provide training and guidelines for AI system developers and operators to ensure accurate and robust disclosures.
  • Collaborate with AI system providers: Work with AI system providers to ensure they understand the city's disclosure policy and can provide accurate and relevant information.
  • Promote transparency: Publicize the availability of AI-related information and encourage citizens to make requests for such information.
