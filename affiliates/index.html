Brand guidelines

Keep it honest, keep it lowercase

  • Write it lowercase. The brand is ghostcorpnet — never "GhostCorpNet", "GhostCorp", or "Kestrelattice".
  • Use your affiliate link, unaltered. Don't shorten, redirect, or mask it — buyers should see they're going to Gumroad.
  • Don't invent outcomes. No testimonials, no revenue claims, no "thousands of teams" — promote what's actually in the products, which every product page documents.
  • Disclose the relationship. Mark affiliate links as affiliate links ("I earn a commission if you buy") — it's the law in most places and it's good practice everywhere.
  • Don't bid on the brand. No paid search ads targeting "ghostcorpnet" as a keyword.
  • No spam. Don't email, DM, or post unsolicited promotions. Recommending to your own audience is the whole point — blasting strangers is how accounts get banned.
  • Prices and titles stay as published. Quote the live Gumroad price and title exactly; don't round, don't rebrand.
FAQ

Questions, answered

Which products earn commission?

All published paid products — the $19 kits, the $29 Studio Edition, the $49 tier, the $79 bundle, the $99 team license, and the $149/$299 tiers. The free ($0) lead magnets earn no commission, but they're a good way to introduce your audience before the paid pitch.

How do I get paid?

Gumroad handles affiliate tracking and payouts directly — your 30% is paid through Gumroad's affiliate system to the payout method on your own Gumroad account. We never touch your commission.

Is there a minimum audience?

No. A newsletter with 200 readers who ship agents beats a million followers who don't. We care about fit, not size.

Can I review a product before promoting it?

Yes — ask in your affiliate request and we'll arrange review access to the product you want to promote. We'd rather you recommend something you've actually read.

Can I write my own copy instead of the swipes?

Please do, if it fits your voice. The swipes are a starting point. The brand rules above still apply — especially no invented claims.
