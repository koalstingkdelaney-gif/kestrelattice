Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Short version: we collect as little as possible. We don't run accounts, we don't track you across the web, and we don't sell data.

What we collect

  • Site analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to see aggregate visits (which pages get viewed, roughly from where). It does not tell us who you are. Measurement ID: G-541TCHWW98. You can block it with any ad blocker or Google's Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Emails you send us. If you email ghostcorpnetai@gmail.com or reply to one of our messages, we keep the thread so we can answer you. Reply "stop" any time and we delete you from outreach permanently.
  • Email list (if you join). If you sign up for updates, we keep your address only to send those updates, and every message has an unsubscribe path.

What we don't collect

No accounts, no passwords, no payment details — purchases and payments are handled entirely by Gumroad, and their privacy policy applies to checkout. We never see your card or bank information.

Cookies

We don't set our own tracking cookies. Google Analytics may set cookies as described in Google's own policies.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent, or share your information with third parties, except the service providers that run the site and store (GitHub Pages, Gumroad, Google Analytics) as needed to operate.

Your rights

Email ghostcorpnetai@gmail.com to ask what we have about you, or to have it deleted. We'll do it.

Changes

We'll update the date above if this policy changes.
